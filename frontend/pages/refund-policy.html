Refund Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Eligibility

Refunds are available within 14 days of payment if work has not yet begun. Once work has started, refunds are not available.

2. Process

Refunds are sent back to the original cryptocurrency address used for payment. Processing time is 1-3 business days.

3. Exceptions

No refunds are available for completed or delivered work. Custom projects may have modified refund terms agreed upon before work begins.

4. Contact

For refund requests, contact support@novapay.services with your Order ID.
